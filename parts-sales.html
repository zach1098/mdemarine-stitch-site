Identification

Get the Part Right First

Engine tags, serial numbers, clear photos, and application details prevent expensive guessing.

  • Engine make, model, serial number, and horsepower
  • Component photos from multiple angles
  • Failure symptoms and reason for replacement
Options

Repair, OEM, or Aftermarket

The best solution may be a direct replacement, a practical aftermarket option, a repair, or a fabricated improvement.

  • Compare available replacement paths
  • Confirm fitment before ordering
  • Coordinate with engine service or fabrication when needed
What to Send

A parts request someone can act on

  • Engine make, model, serial number, horsepower, and vessel application
  • Part number, casting number, or component name if known
  • Clear photos of the engine tag and the component from multiple angles
  • Whether an OEM part is required or a proven aftermarket option is acceptable
  • Required timeline, vessel location, and whether the boat is currently down
More Than a Parts Counter

When the failed part points to a larger problem

If the root failure is still unclear, the engine service process comes first. If the original design or mounting arrangement is the problem, custom fabrication may be the better answer.
