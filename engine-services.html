Service Scope

Clear diagnostics and a realistic pre-repair evaluation help determine whether the right answer is routine service, onboard component work, or a deeper engine or transmission rebuild.

Diagnostics

Problem Calls

Hands-on troubleshooting for engines that are not starting, running, cooling, or producing power as they should.

  • No-start, low-power, smoke, and overheating complaints
  • Oil, coolant, fuel, noise, and vibration concerns
  • Systematic testing before parts are replaced
Repairs

Engine Services

Mechanical service and repair work scaled to the condition of the engine, transmission, and onboard components.

  • General maintenance
  • Onboard component repair and replacement, including alternators, starters, water pumps, and injectors
  • In-frame and out-of-frame engine and transmission rebuilds and replacement
Execution

Boat Yard Coordination

For repair work that depends on yard access, scheduling, lifting, outside trades, or other vessel systems.

  • Haul-out and schedule-sensitive work
  • Crane, lift, and access coordination
  • Pre-repair evaluation
Before You Call

Information that speeds up diagnosis

  • Engine make, model, serial number, horsepower, and hours if known
  • Current symptoms, recent alarms, and what changed before the issue began
  • Photos of the engine room, service access, engine tag, and affected components
  • Whether the vessel is in the water, hauled out, or scheduled with a boat yard
  • Recent maintenance, repairs, or parts already replaced
