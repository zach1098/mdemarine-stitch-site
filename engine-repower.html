Repower Process

Plan the fit before committing to the installation

Engine dimensions are only one piece of a repower. The plan also needs to account for transmission selection, engine-room access, mounts, cooling, exhaust, controls, hull systems, lifting, and boat-yard sequencing.

Planning

Getting the Project Started

  • Repower pre-installation checklist
  • Consulting with engine and transmission suppliers to ensure proper fitment and application
  • Coordinating and assisting with hull and other vessel systems as needed
Installation

Fabrication and Repower Integration

  • Custom engine mount estimates
  • 3D scanning and CAD modeling of the engine room for accurate measurements and layout
  • Coordination of fabrication, access, and installation requirements before work begins
Project Integration

Mechanical work and marine fabrication go hand in hand

Every repower introduces fitment questions. MDE Marine can connect the mechanical plan with custom engine mounts, cooler mounting, brackets, plumbing changes, and the other fabricated pieces required to make the new package work in the existing vessel.

What to Have Ready

Start with the vessel and power-package details

  • Vessel make, model, length, location, and current boat-yard status
  • Existing engine and transmission make, model, horsepower, and serial numbers
  • Candidate replacement engine and transmission information, if already selected
  • Engine-room, access-path, mount, exhaust, cooling, and control photos
  • Target schedule and any known haul-out, lift, or supplier deadlines
